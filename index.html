Civil Appeal 4821/2025High ImpactSupreme CourtDecided: Apr 2, 2026
Source: Supreme Court of India ↗

AI Summary

The Supreme Court held that stock transfers between distinct persons (branches of the same entity registered in different states) constitute a "supply" under GST and ITC must be reversed proportionately. This overturns the Gujarat HC ruling and creates binding precedent. 5 of your clients with multi-state registrations may be impacted and need to re-examine their inter-branch transfer accounting.

Key Holdings
Inter-branch transfers between distinct persons are taxable supplies under Section 7(1)(c) of CGST Act.
ITC already availed on such transfers must be reversed in the current return period with applicable interest.
The receiving branch can avail fresh ITC on the inward supply, if the transfer is properly invoiced under GST.
